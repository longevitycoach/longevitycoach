### The Wake-Up Call: Cholesterol and Health Metrics

My diagnosis showed elevated cholesterol levels. My doctor recommended getting my body mass index below 25 and body fat below 24%.

I was inspired by [Atomic Habits](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits) by James Clear to make lasting changes to my lifestyle.

### Implementing Lifestyle Changes

I made several lifestyle changes:

- Added running and morning exercise to my routine
- Eliminated red meat from my diet
- Increased intake of fiber, fruits, and vegetables

These changes helped stabilize my LDL levels and improve my HDL. I've maintained a BMI of 26 and body fat of 27%.

## The Science Behind Personalized Health Coaching

### Understanding Biomarkers

Biomarkers are measurable indicators of the severity or presence of some disease state. They can be:

- **Blood-based**: Cholesterol levels, glucose, etc.
- **Physical**: Blood pressure, heart rate, etc.
- **Lifestyle-based**: Sleep patterns, activity levels, etc.

### The Role of AI in Health Coaching

Modern health coaching leverages AI to:

1. Analyze complex biomarker data
2. Identify patterns and correlations
3. Provide personalized recommendations
4. Adapt to changing health status
